# Changelog

Written for the people who use HumSukhan, not for the people who wrote it.

## 1.0.0

The first release. HumSukhan is a communication assistant for Deaf and
hard-of-hearing people, in English and Urdu.

### It works the moment you install it

Captions and the speaking voice run on your own phone. There is nothing to set
up, no key to enter, and no account needed to start — and because none of it
goes through a server, it keeps working on a train or with the signal off.

If your phone does not yet have the language you want, HumSukhan says so in one
sentence and offers one button. Your phone does the download, HumSukhan checks
it really worked, and then carries on with whatever you were doing — you do not
have to find your way back to it. You are never asked to go hunting through
settings for a speech engine.

Some phones cannot add a language from inside an app. On those, HumSukhan tells
you plainly instead of showing you a button that does nothing.

### Everyday conversation

See what the person in front of you is saying, and answer in your own words.

- Live captions while the other person speaks.
- A natural pause finishes the sentence you were reading and **keeps
  listening** — you do not tap between sentences.
- Choose how long a pause should be: short (1.2s), natural (1.7s), patient
  (2.5s), or only when you say so.
- Reply by typing, by tapping a quick phrase, or by having the app say it out
  loud for you.
- The keyboard switches to Urdu and Nastaliq as soon as you type Urdu, and
  Roman Urdu is written into Urdu script.
- Scroll back to re-read something and you stay there. New captions no longer
  drag you to the bottom mid-sentence; a **Latest** button brings you back when
  you want it.
- Save or delete each conversation when you finish it.

### Meetings, lectures and classes

Capture the whole thing, then understand it.

- A complete transcript. Half-heard words never appear — only finished
  sentences are written down.
- **Pause** for the coffee break and **resume** into the same transcript. One
  lecture stays one lecture, and the time you spent paused is not counted as
  time you recorded.
- Long sessions survive a dropped connection: you are told it is reconnecting,
  and nothing already captured is lost.
- A summary with key points, action items, who was named and when things are
  due — generated from the full transcript, and always marked as AI-written so
  you can check it against the words themselves.
- Share or export any session.
- Everything is deleted automatically after the number of days you choose, up
  to 15, with the countdown shown on every session.

### Sounds you cannot hear

- Listens for a siren, an alarm, breaking glass, a doorbell, a knock, a baby
  crying, a dog, a vehicle horn, and a ringing phone.
- Alerts you by vibration, an on-screen alert, a screen flash, or the camera
  light — as many as you want at once.
- Keeps listening while you use other apps, with a Quick Settings tile to turn
  it on and off from anywhere.
- **All of this happens on your phone.** No audio is ever uploaded.

### Everything else

- Dark mode, and a separate high-contrast theme in black and white.
- Large text that adds to your phone's own text size rather than replacing it,
  plus a caption size you can set on its own.
- The whole app in English or Urdu, including every error message.
- Sign up with an email and a password. No verification email to wait for.
- Where HumSukhan is installed without a server behind it, it simply opens on
  your own phone instead of asking you to sign in to something that isn't
  there. Your conversations stay on the phone and nothing is uploaded.
- Your data is yours: everything is scoped to your account, and signing out
  leaves nothing behind for the next person to sign in.

### When something goes wrong

Every failure says what happened and what you can do about it — on screen, in
your language. Nothing in HumSukhan tells you anything by sound alone.
